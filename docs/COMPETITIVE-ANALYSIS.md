## Where Ozark Junk Removals can win
1. **City-level landing pages.** Neither verified local competitor has dedicated pages for Nixa, Ozark, Republic, etc. — we ship 8, with unique content (flag the 4 thinner ones for expansion).
2. **Garage-sale leftover pickup.** No competitor offers anything similar. It's a unique offer for yard signs, Facebook groups and Marketplace ads.
3. **Lower-friction quoting.** Visual load-size picker + phone-photo upload + 3-step form on every service and city page. Competitors use a basic contact form or phone.
4. **Speed & polish.** Static site with Lighthouse mobile scores of 97 performance and 100 accessibility, best practices and SEO (tested locally — see TESTING.md).
5. **Honesty as positioning.** Competitors lean on review counts and big claims. Until Ozark has reviews, the site wins on clarity: clear process, clear exclusions, no fake trust badges.

## Where competitors are stronger (act on these)
1. **Reviews.** 146–238 Google reviews is the bar. Without a review engine, Ozark will lose map-pack clicks even with a better site. → Ask every customer; send a review link by text after each job.
2. **Google Business Profile.** Competitors have mature profiles with photos. → Set up the GBP immediately, add trailer/crew photos weekly.
3. **Service breadth pages.** Easy Cleanouts ranks for long-tail niches (eviction, probate, fix & flip). → Add pages like "Eviction & Rental Cleanouts" or "Probate Property Cleanouts" once real experience backs them.
4. **Real photos.** Junk B Gone shows before/after jobs. → Photograph every job from day one.
